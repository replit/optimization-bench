# Project Gutenberg texts: copyright status outside the United States

This notice supersedes the per-record `license` text in
`workload/driver/small-text.json` (75 public development texts) and in the
held-out `tests/heldout/small-text-sources.json` (65 validation texts). Those
records say the Project Gutenberg public-domain statement applies to the
United States and that other jurisdictions require review. The records are
hash-pinned, so they are left unchanged and this notice records the review.

Check performed on 2026-11-17: every credited contributor (author,
illustrator or editor) in the Project Gutenberg catalogue record of all 140
texts has a recorded death year of 1946 or earlier. The latest is H. G. Wells
(1866-1946); the latest in the 65 validation texts is Virginia Woolf
(1882-1941). All 140 texts are English-language originals, not translations.

The literary works are therefore in the public domain in the United States and
in every country whose copyright term is the author's life plus 70 years or
shorter (since 1 January 2017 at the latest). Countries with longer terms are
not covered by this check.

Each text is distributed unmodified, with its complete Project Gutenberg header
and licence; the Project Gutenberg trademark terms are retained in
`PROJECT-GUTENBERG-TERMS.txt`.
